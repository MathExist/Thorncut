Your last life is your best backup

When you die in Thornshot, that life comes back as an Echo.

It reruns your attempt beside you, shoots whatever is near and explodes where you fell.

A retry keeps the same map, so your Echoes know the way. Die in the right place and your past lives clear the road for the next one.

Run, dash and shoot your way through

Dash through enemies

Cut straight through an enemy and your dash comes back at once. Landing on heads refills it too.

Break the thorn walls

Red thorns block the way until you clear enough of the enemies in that stretch.

Ride what you find

Boats, gliders, zip lines and rail cars carry you over toxic rivers and through tunnels.

Pick from 10 characters

Start with six and unlock the rest by defeating enemies.

Climb the Style rank

Mix kills, stomps, dash slashes and perfect reloads to go from D to S. Each rank adds damage, and a hit knocks you back down.

Choose your difficulty

Six levels run from First Timer to Max, each with its own save.
